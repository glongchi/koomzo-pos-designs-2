Salon · Part 5

When something goes wrong

A client is late and the next one is waiting.

Start the waiting client if a chair is free; drag the late booking later. Koomzo warns if it now overlaps.

A stylist is absent.

Book → their column → move each booking to someone who can do the service. The client can be told by WhatsApp. Messages soon

A colour job ran over.

Drag the end of the booking. The next booking shows the clash; move it or start it with another stylist.

A price dispute at payment.

The ticket shows the service and its listed price. A discount needs a reason; above the limit, a manager.

The product ran out.

It still sells; stock shows below zero until the delivery is received. Set a reorder point in Inventory.
