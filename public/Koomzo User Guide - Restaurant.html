Restaurant & Bar · Part 1

Is this for me?

Restaurant & Bar suits any place where people eat or drink — at a table, at the bar, or to take away. Setup offers a starting layout for each:

Maquis / small restaurant

Salle and terrasse. The most common start.

Restaurant

Salle, terrasse and a bar counter.

Bar / lounge

Stools at the bar and lounge tables.

Hotel restaurant

Restaurant, bar and room service.

Snack / fast food

Order at the counter, a few tables to eat in.

Counter only

No tables — tabs and counter tickets.

Restaurant & Bar · Part 2

Set up Lite

About an hour, once. Do it before your first service.

  1. Getting startedAccount, location and staff — see P1.
  2. Choose a layoutSetup → Floor plan → Start from a template. Maquis (Salle + Terrasse) is the usual choice. Set the number of tables in each area before you apply.
  3. Add your menuSetup → Menu → Start from a template for a ready-made menu you then edit — or create your categories, then each dish with its price and the station that prepares it — grill, sauce pot, bar. Step by step: Menu.
  4. Choose paymentsCash is on. Add MTN MoMo and Orange Money with your merchant numbers.
  5. Choose your devicesOne device to start. Add phones, a kitchen screen and a kiosk later — P4.
  6. Do a test orderSeat a table, order, send, mark it ready, pay and clear. Then you're ready to open.

Start from a Koomzo template

Templates (under the categories, or the first button on an empty menu) opens sample menus for your kind of venue — Maquis, Bar, Snack, Hotel. Tap one to see every dish and price, then Use this template. If you already have dishes, choose Add to mine (same-name dishes are updated) or Replace. You get your own copy: Koomzo never changes it afterwards. Sample prices carry a Check price label until you edit the dish or tap Confirm all prices. Changed your mind? Undo works until your next edit. The same button on the Floor plan opens sample layouts, and a menu offers its matching layout.

The whole menu at once: CSV file

Under the categories, CSV template downloads a file ready to fill in Excel or Google Sheets: one row per dish, columns category, name, description, price, station, tags, image (image is an optional https:// photo link). Category, name and price are required; unknown categories are created. Then Import CSV: Koomzo shows the dishes added, updated and any rows skipped with their row number, before applying. Add and update keeps the rest of the menu; Replace whole menu clears it. Export current menu saves today's menu in the same format.

Customer display Lite

A screen turned towards the customer — the rear screen of an all-in-one POS, a tablet, or an Android payment terminal — shows each line as you ring it up, the total, and how to pay.

  1. Pair itSetup → Customer display shows a 4-digit code. On the tablet or terminal, open Koomzo Customer Display and type the code. On an all-in-one POS with a built-in rear screen, tap Open on second screen instead — no code.
  2. Check itShow test screen puts a sample order on the display for 8 seconds. The register shows Display in green when it is connected.
  3. Choose what customers can tapLoyalty number, receipt choice (WhatsApp, SMS, printed, none) and tipping — each has its own switch. Whatever the customer taps arrives on your register as a suggestion: Accept or Ignore. Nothing is applied on its own.
  4. Set the welcome screenA welcome line and up to 3 promo slides, shown when no one is being served. Pick French or English for the display — it doesn't change your own screens.

What the customer never sees

Costs, kitchen notes, void reasons, staff names and PINs, other tables or held tickets, full phone numbers. A removed line shows struck through for a moment, then goes. If the display loses the register for 5 seconds it greys the total and shows Reconnecting… rather than an old amount.

Tables: a counter till moves between tables all evening, so a table’s bill appears on the display only when you open Print bill or Pay for it. Counter, takeaway and tab orders show while you ring them up. Change it in Setup → Customer display → Table orders.

Restaurant & Bar · Part 5

When something goes wrong

The power went off in the middle of service.

Keep working on battery. Every order is already saved. When power returns, open the app — open tables come back as they were.

No internet, and a guest wants to pay by MoMo.

Take cash, or keep the bill open and confirm the MoMo when the internet returns. Everything else carries on.

The main device died.

Phones keep taking orders and hold them. Charge it and turn it on — or a manager makes another device the main one. Soon

A guest left before the food came out.

Clear the table and choose Cancel. It's logged as a void and shown to the kitchen.

The cook forgot to tap Served.

When you clear the table, choose Already served. Served tickets also leave the screen by themselves after 10 minutes.

We put the order on the wrong table.

Open the order → Move → pick the right table. The kitchen ticket is relabelled.

A server says they didn't void that dish.

Setup → Service rules → Today's voids shows who voided what, when, why, and who approved.

The sync light shows "needs attention".

A change couldn't be accepted by the cloud. The manager opens it and chooses what to keep. Nothing is lost.
