Code changes the register spec implies

Eleven changes, now all implemented — see Delivered for what landed and where it departed from this plan. Four of them were defects against decisions the platform has already made rather than new work. The headline finding: Restaurant & Bar is the correct implementation. It reads the shared tender list, uses the shared TVA label, and gates room charge on a live capability. Retail, Retail Flex and Mobile each restate those things locally, and drift where they restate.

Verdict

4 defects

Code that contradicts something the platform already decided — hardcoded tender lists, fractional francs, USD-era cash denominations, duplicated tax rate. These are cheap and should be fixed regardless of the spec.

6 gaps

Behaviour the spec's §19–20 decisions require and no register has yet: pending MoMo state, a tenders array, structured ticket numbers, discount ceilings, hold-blocking close, the Prepare capability.

1 consolidation

Retail and Retail Flex converge. Deliberately last — no customer-visible gain, and it is much easier after the four defects are fixed in both.

1 reference

Restaurant's ChargeSheet is the pattern to copy. Do not rewrite it — lift it into a shared component and delete the three local copies.

Delivered

All eleven changes are implemented across the four registers. What follows is the audit as written, kept for the reasoning; this section records what actually landed and where it departed from the plan.

11 of 11 changes in. Four new platform files now own what the registers used to restate — kz/kz-tender.js (list, phases, persistence), kz/kz-charge.jsx (the charge model and shared body), kz/kz-ticket.js (numbering) and kz/kz-policy.js (discount ceilings). retail/ is retired and the sync trees are gone.

Where the build departed from the plan

Change 1 became a shared model, not one shared sheet. The plan said to lift Restaurant's ChargeSheet into a single component. The three registers have genuinely different CSS vocabularies (.tender, .m-tender, .sheet), so the model is shared via useCharge and Retail and Flex share the body, while Mobile and Restaurant keep their own markup around the same rules. No register owns a rule; three own their chrome.
The unhappy phases render but are not clickable. declined, expired and unknown all have panels and copy, and unknown has its status-query path. But the simulated provider callback always succeeds after 3.6 s, so nothing in the UI reaches them. Worth a tweak toggle before this is used to demo failure handling.
Hospitality hand-over is only in retail's close screen. Decision 20.6's transfer-to-next-shift matters most for a table still eating at shift change, but Restaurant has no shift screen in this module. The retail Hand over button is the mechanism; the hospitality case still needs the floor-plan answer flagged earlier.
Prepare is data only. The capability exists and can be switched, but no screen consumes it yet — routing a non-hospitality line to a station is the build behind it, and it is deliberately after launch.
Two numbers are still placeholders. The discount ceilings and the 5 000 F refund-approval threshold are my defaults, not derived from a real tenant's margins — and they belong in tenant settings rather than kz-policy.js constants.

The duplicate trees — removed

The register sources used to exist in five places — the canonical tree, plus koomzo-pos-web/public/…, _sync-bundle/, _sync-inventory/, sync-to-local/ and sync-transfers/. Patching those copies individually is how the files drifted apart, and it is why Restaurant was right while three registers were wrong. All 275 files were deleted at the user's direction; the design-system tree is now the single source. The changes below were made there once, and nowhere else.

Change 1 · Delete the hardcoded tender lists

Change 2 · Fractional francs in retail totals

2Tax and total computed to two decimals
Defect Done
retail/rt-app.jsx:71–72retailflex/rx-app.jsxretail/rt-screens.jsx:125 · KeypadSheet

Invariant 8 of the spec, and the locale file's own comment, say a price with a decimal point is a bug. The retail totals memo produces one on nearly every ticket, and the keypad offers a . key for price entry.

Today
const tax = +(net * profile.tax).toFixed(2); return { … tax, total: +(net + tax).toFixed(2) }; // keypad line.price.toFixed(2)
Target
const tax = Math.round(net * KZ_LOCALE.vat / 100); return { … tax, total: Math.round(net) + tax }; // keypad: no decimal key for price String(Math.round(line.price))

Keep the decimal key for weight (kilos legitimately have three places) and remove it in price and discount modes. Weight quantities stay fractional; money never is.

Change 3 · Quick-cash keys use denominations that don't exist

3Rounding to the next 5 / 10 / 20
Defect Done
retail/rt-screens.jsx:184

The keys offer the total rounded up to 5, 10 and 20 — a dollar-till habit. A Cameroonian customer pays with a 1 000, 2 000, 5 000 or 10 000 F note. KZ_LOCALE.notes already lists exactly those.

Today
[total, ceil(total/5)*5, ceil(total/10)*10, ceil(total/20)*20]
Target
[total, ...KZ_LOCALE.notes .filter(n => n >= total).slice(0,3)] // plus: next round 500 above total

Change due should be checked against KZ_LOCALE.coins so the cashier is warned when exact change is impossible — the 5 F floor is the reason that list exists.

Change 4 · Tax rate duplicated per profile

4Each profile carries its own tax number
Defect Done
retail/rt-data.jsretailflex/rx-data.jssalon/sl-data.js:129retailflex/rx-parts.jsx:179

TVA is national and single-rated. Profiles carrying tax: 0.1925 are five copies of one national number, and Flex rebuilds the label by formatting that float back into a string. If the rate ever moves, this is five edits and a formatting bug waiting to happen.

Today · rx-parts.jsx
TVA ({String(+(profile.tax*100) .toFixed(2)).replace('.',',')} %)
Target
{KZ_LOCALE.vatLabel} // profile keeps only: zeroRated: true|false

A profile should express whether it is taxed (fresh food, some pharmacy lines are zero-rated), never at what rate.

Change 5 · Model the MoMo pending state

5Every charge sheet jumps straight to Paid
DoneNew · spec §19
kz/kz-tender.js · phases, provisioningkz/kz-charge.jsx · useCharge (new)

All four sheets are const [paid, setPaid] = useState(false) — a boolean. On a push rail the register requests payment and then waits for the customer to authorise on their own handset. A boolean cannot express that, and cannot express a timeout, a decline, or an unknown outcome needing a status query.

Today
const [paid, setPaid] = useState(false); onClick={() => setPaid(true)}
Target
// 'idle' | 'requested' | 'pending' // | 'succeeded' | 'declined' // | 'expired' | 'unknown' const [phase, setPhase] = useState('idle');

UI this unlocks: a pending panel with the amount locked and a countdown; Cancel & switch tender as the only escape (never a manual "mark paid" while a push is live); expired presented as an ordinary retry, not an error. Also needs the three provisioning modes from §19 — push, attest, manual — as a tenant capability, since attest is the only one that works offline.

Change 6 · A ticket holds several tenders

6Split payment is a label with nothing behind it
DoneGap · decision 20.2
retail/rt-app.jsxrestaurant/store.jsxmobile/m-app.jsx

Retail's tender sheet offers Split payment and then shows a hint — there is no data structure for a second tender. Decision 20.2 requires each capture to persist as it happens, so a restart mid-split resumes with money already taken intact.

Today
const [tender, setTender] = useState('card'); // one string, one payment, no balance
Target
tenders: [{ id, kind, amount, ref, phase, at, by }] balance = total − sum(succeeded) // persisted on every capture

This is the one structural change on the list, and the reason it sits in the "before launch" bucket: changing the shape of a money record after real receipts exist means migrating them. A resumed part-tendered ticket must also lock line editing — the total is already frozen.

Change 7 · Structured ticket numbers

7Order #1042 everywhere
DoneGap · decision 20.4
retail/rt-parts.jsx:148retailflex/rx-parts.jsx:155restaurant/modals.jsx:50mobile/m-components.jsx

A bare sequence collides the moment two tills sell offline. Decision 20.4 composes the number at the till from location, register, session and sequence, so collisions are structurally impossible and the server never renumbers a receipt a customer is holding.

Put the composer in kz/kz-locale.js or a new kz/kz-ticket.js next to the other platform-wide formats, so all four registers and the receipt print read one function. Display the short form in tight spaces; print and store the full one.

Change 8 · Discount ceilings and approval

8Any cashier can discount any line to zero
DoneGap · decision 20.3
retail/rt-screens.jsx · KeypadSheetrestaurant/register-parts.jsxmobile/m-components.jsx · MEditor

The keypad accepts a discount percentage with no ceiling and no approval, and price override is available to whoever is at the till. Decision 20.3 sets a line ceiling (default 10 %) and a ticket ceiling (5 % or 5 000 F), with supervisor approval and a reason above it.

Two tenant settings, one approval sheet, one reason list. The shift report then needs a discount as % of net sales figure — the number an owner can act on, and the reason the ceiling exists at all.

Change 9 · Held tickets block the Z close

9Shift close ignores open tickets entirely
DoneGap · decision 20.6
retail/rt-screens.jsx · ShiftView

ShiftView shows takings by tender and a cash count, and offers Close — with no reference to held or part-tendered tickets. Decision 20.6 requires the close to list them and refuse to complete until each is settled, voided, or transferred to the next shift.

Add a To resolve card above the cash count, a bulk auto-void that is supervisor-only and writes a reason per ticket, and — for hospitality — transfer-to-next-shift with explicit reassignment of ownership. Also: the Z report must reconcile cash and each wallet separately, since MoMo takings never enter the drawer and must not inflate expected cash.

Change 10 · The Prepare capability

10Station routing exists only inside Restaurant
DoneGap · decision 20.7
kz/kz-caps.js · retail, grocery, salon

Decision 20.7 keeps one routing model and exposes it to non-hospitality trades as Prepare, off by default. Today the retail and grocery module entries in kz-caps.js have no such capability, so a bakery or butcher counter has no way to route work.

Add prepare to the retail, grocery and salon cap lists, default off, at mid tier. The copy adapts per trade — Send to stylist, Send to counter — but the object underneath stays the one kitchen ticket, so the production display is built once.

Change 11 · Retail and Flex converge

11Two retail registers, one product
DoneConsolidation · decision 20.1
retail/* → retailflex/*kz/kz-caps.js already points at Flex

Worth noting: kz-caps.js already names Koomzo POS - Retail Flex.html as the retail module's file. The platform has effectively chosen Flex; the Retail tree is a second copy that still has to be maintained and, as this audit shows, has quietly diverged.

Fold Retail's six profiles into Flex's preset list, keep Flex's editable feature map as the only model, and retire retail/. Do it after changes 1–4, so the defects are fixed once rather than merged twice.
