One register · four compositions · the phone three

Where the ticket lives on a 390 px screen

Three answers to one question, all running the same Retail Flex register — same presets, same qualifier chain, same tender model, same approval step. Only the ticket's home differs. For a shop owner whose phone is the till, this is not a preview convenience; it is the layout they will look at every day for a year.

BarphoneOrder: bar · default

A summary pinned above the rail — total and count always visible. The ticket opens as a sheet over it.

Why it is the default

The running total never leaves the screen, which is what a solo shopkeeper actually watches. Two surfaces means one extra tap to edit a line — the trade for always knowing the number.

SheetphoneOrder: sheet

One surface. The ticket peeks at the bottom and is pulled up by its head; the catalogue stays behind it.

Where it wins

Fewest surfaces and the most direct reach — the ticket is one drag away and edits happen in place. Costs a little catalogue height to the peek, and the collapsed lip shows less than the bar does.

TabphoneOrder: tabs

No overlay at all. Catalogue and ticket are two full views behind a tab strip, with a count on the ticket tab.

Where it wins

The ticket gets the whole screen, so long orders and the qualifier chain have room. The cost is that the total is out of sight while adding items — the tab badge is all you get.

What this replaces

These three patterns came from the separate Mobile register, which had its own catalogue, its own components and its own stylesheet — and, because of that, no presets, no capability map and none of the qualifier chain. A phone could not age-check a sale, take a pharmacy sign-off, pick a variant, choose a batch, capture a serial or weigh anything.

They now live in Flex as a tenant choice, so all three carry the whole register. That matters more here than it would elsewhere: in this market the phone is frequently the only till in the business, so a reduced phone register is a reduced business — not a convenience tier.

Switch between them in Flex's own panel under Phone: ticket lives in, or pin one with ?device=phone&phoneOrder=sheet as these frames do. The convergence plan records what moved and what was deliberately left behind.
