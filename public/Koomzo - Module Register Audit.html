Verdict — as audited

Everything from here to the end of the gaps describes the state before this work, in the past tense where it matters. All six gaps are now closed — Delivered records what changed. The audit is kept because it is the reasoning, not because it is current.

The one that actually mattered: a tenant running a salon and a shop had two different discount policies. Flex enforced a 10 % line ceiling with a logged supervisor approval; Salon's register said "Discounts over 20% need a manager PIN" — as a sentence in the UI, with no approval step behind it, and the chips let a stylist apply 50 % in one tap. Same company, same owner, two answers, and only one of them auditable. Fixed: Salon's chips now derive from its own ceiling and anything above it opens a real approval.

Two modules conformed

Flex and Restaurant already read all five platform files — tender list, phases, persistence, numbering, policy. The other four now do too.

Salon was half-migrated

It read KZ_LOCALE.tenderList and formatted money through the locale — then duplicated the tax rate, the threshold and the ticket number. All three are now the platform's.

Three shared nothing

Gym, Hotel and Grocery each defined xaf() — the same function, three times — and each carried its own tender list. All three now read window.money and extend KZ_TENDER.

But Gym already knows

Gym is the only module outside the registers that handles an offline wallet payment: "Recorded now · the mobile-money confirmation is matched on sync." That is the attest path, described in copy but never modelled.

Delivered

All six gaps are implemented. The audit below is kept as written — it is the reasoning — and this section records what changed and what the two configurable answers became.

6 of 6 gaps in. Salon, Gym, Hotel and Grocery now read the same platform as the two registers. Three copies of xaf() and four tender lists are gone; money is non-breaking suite-wide; and kz-policy.js gained per-module overrides so tax liability and the discount ceiling are settings rather than assumptions.

The two configurable answers

QuestionHow it is configured
TVA liabilityKZ_POLICY.modules[id].taxable. Gym ships false — deliberately, since nobody has confirmed the treatment, and a visible zero is easier to challenge than a silent 19,25 %. Set it true and every gym figure picks up the national rate with no other edit. Note the distinction the code carries: zero-rated is a rate of zero on a taxable supply, which is a different line on a return from a supply outside scope.
Discount ceilingKZ_POLICY.modules[id].lineDiscountPct. Salon 20 % (service margins, and regulars), Grocery 5 % (thin margins, high volume), everything else the tenant base of 10 %. The chips a module offers are derived from its ceiling, so the UI can never present a discount the policy forbids.

Restaurant line discounts — decided: no

You said you didn't think so, and I agree, for a reason worth writing down: comping a dish is not a discount. A discount reprices a line before it is made. A comp happens after the kitchen has cooked it and the stock is gone — the money changes but the cost does not, and recording it as a discount would quietly understate food cost and overstate margin. It belongs as a void-with-reason on a fired line, which is a different object with a different audit trail, and it should be specified as that rather than bolted onto the discount path.

Hotel & Grocery — the cheap ones · all fixed

Both defined the same xaf() and both carried their own tender list. Grocery's till also has no ticket numbering and no discount policy; Hotel's folio numbering is its own thing and should stay, because a folio is not a ticket — it is an account that accrues over several nights and settles once.

Neither has a qualifier chain or a discount path, so they are three-line changes plus a tender-list swap. They are in the plan because leaving them is how the suite drifted in the first place.

Restaurant — conforms, with one honest hole

Restaurant reads all five platform files. The one thing it lacks is a line discount path at all, so KZ_POLICY has nothing to govern there. That is a missing feature, not a conformance gap — but it means a server cannot discount a bill, which for a restaurant comping a dish is a real omission worth raising separately.
