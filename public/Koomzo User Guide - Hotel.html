Hotel

Every room, its state and who is in it tonight — bookings, the guest's bill, cleaning and the night audit, on the desk laptop and the housekeepers' phones. Shared chapters — devices, offline, payments — are in the Platform chapters.

Hotel · Part 1

Is this for me?

Hotel suits any place that sells a bed for the night or a flat by the month. Setup asks which shape you are:

Small hotel · auberge · motel

10 to 40 rooms sold by the night. The most common start.

Apartments · furnished flats

Units let by the week or the month, with water and power billed on the meter.

Lodge · both

Rooms and apartments under one roof, often with a restaurant and bar.

What you get at each level

LiteSell the rooms

  • Room rack: every room, tonight
  • Bookings, arrivals, departures
  • Guest records with CNI or passport
  • Cleaning list and staff roster

MidThe bill and the law

  • Folio: charges on the room
  • Rates, availability, OTA bookings
  • Police register and taxe de séjour
  • Pre-arrival card · restaurant charges
  • Rooms off sale for repairs

FullClose the day, grow

  • Night audit and day close
  • Deposits and damage
  • Long stays and apartments
  • Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR
  • Minimum stay, closed to arrival

Hotel · Part 2

Set up Lite

About an hour and a half, once. Do it before your first guest.

  1. Getting startedAccount, location and staff — see P1. Give housekeepers the Housekeeping role: their phone shows only the cleaning list.
  2. Choose your shapeSmall hotel, apartments, or a lodge with both.
  3. Add room types and roomsStandard, Superior, Suite, Apartment — each with its nightly rate. Then each room: number, floor, type.
  4. Set the house rulesCheck-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00, the taxe de séjour per guest per night, and VAT.
  5. Booking formSetup → Booking form: the longest stay the desk can enter, the deposit step, and whether the room list follows the chosen dates.
  6. PaymentsCash on; add MTN MoMo and Orange Money. Card when your terminal is ready.
  7. Test a stayBook a room for tonight, check in, post a charge, check out and clean the room.

Hotel · Part 4

Screens

The left rail: Rooms, Bookings, Cleaning, Folios, Guests, Long stay, Rates, Audit, Reports, Team, Pre-arrival, and Setup at the bottom. A housekeeper signing in on a phone sees only Rooms, Cleaning and Team.

Rooms Lite

1234567
  1. Rail — the badges count what waits on someone today.
  2. Off sale — a room taken out for repairs, with the reason.
  3. Tonight — occupancy, arrivals, departures, rooms ready, money on the rooms.
  4. Filters — Arrivals, Departures, Vacant ready, To clean, Long stay.
  5. Room tile — colour is the state; the button is the next thing to do (check in, check out, clean).
  6. To clean — opens the cleaning board.
  7. New booking — see below.

New booking Lite

12345
  1. Guest and mobile — family name first, as on the CNI.
  2. CNI or passport — needed for the police register. Mid
  3. Room — greyed rooms are taken on the dates you chose, not just tonight.
  4. Arrives — today, tomorrow or the next five days in one tap.
  5. Pick a date — a calendar up to a year ahead. The stay shows as a range; booked nights for that room are crossed out; a dot shows how full the house is.

Further down: Nights and Security deposit each have − / + buttons and a box you can type in — any number of nights, any deposit amount. Weekly and monthly rates move a week or a month at a time.

Folios Mid

12345
  1. Outstanding — every running bill, added up.
  2. One line per room — tap to see each charge: room nights, restaurant, bar, laundry, levy.
  3. Balance and what is already paid.
  4. Post a charge — laundry, minibar, a taxi paid for the guest.
  5. Check out & settle — cash, MoMo, Orange Money or split; deposit returned or part kept for damage. Full

Cleaning Lite

123456
  1. Board and Maintenance — rooms to turn round, and faults to fix. Mid
  2. To clean — departures first, then stay-overs; linen changes flagged.
  3. Start — the housekeeper taps when they go in.
  4. Assign — give a room to someone on shift.
  5. Done — the room goes to inspection, then back on sale.
  6. Send the board — each housekeeper's phone gets their list.

Pre-arrival Mid

12345
  1. Cards to verify — check the ID photo against the number once.
  2. Card state — sent, opened, to verify, verified, or filled at the desk.
  3. Verify — at check-in the register line writes itself; nobody retypes a passport.
  4. Guest view — what the guest sees on their phone.
  5. Send link — by WhatsApp. Available soon

Night audit Full

12345
  1. Still open — arrivals not checked in, departures not closed. Post anyway and they roll to tomorrow with a note.
  2. Rooms not turned round — carried to the morning board.
  3. Tonight's figures — rooms sold, room revenue, taxe de séjour.
  4. What will post — one line per room per night. Yesterday is never edited.
  5. Post the night — and the day is closed.

Hotel · Part 5

When something goes wrong

The guest has no CNI or passport on them.

Check them in; the room shows No ID and the audit lists it. Capture the ID before the night audit — the police register needs it.

A Booking.com guest arrives for a room we already sold.

With the room list following dates, the desk can't sell a booked night. If it happens with a hand-entered OTA booking, rebook one of the guests into a free room of the same type.

The power went off during the night audit.

Nothing posts twice. Turn the laptop back on and press Post the night again; rooms already posted are skipped.

Damage found at check-out.

Settle → keep part of the deposit with a reason. The guest's receipt shows it. Full

A room breaks with a guest arriving tonight.

Cleaning → Maintenance → log the fault and take the room off sale. The booking shows a warning; move the guest to another room.

No internet at check-out, and the guest pays by MoMo.

Take cash, or keep the folio open and confirm the MoMo when the internet returns. Check-in and cleaning carry on as normal.
