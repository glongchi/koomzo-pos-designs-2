Module guide

Inventory

One catalogue and one stock count for every module that sells goods — the shop till, the bar, the salon's back-bar, the gym's juice bar. Every change to stock leaves a row you can read. Shared chapters are in the Platform chapters; how modules feed stock is in P7.

Inventory · Part 1

Is this for me?

Switch Inventory on when you want to know what you have, what you sold and what went missing. Without it, the till still sells; with it, every sale takes stock off.

Shops and pharmacies

Count on hand per item, reorder before the shelf is empty, batch and expiry.

Bars and restaurants

Crates and bottles; dishes that use ingredients. Start with drinks only.

Salons and gyms

Retail products sold, and back-bar colour or juice-bar stock used.

What you get at each level

Inventory · Part 2

Set up Lite

Start with the items that matter most — usually drinks, or your top 50 products. Add the rest later.

  1. Import or add itemsItems → Import from a spreadsheet, or Add item: name, SKU or barcode, price, cost, unit (each, crate, ml, kg).
  2. Choose what is trackedTrack stock for goods; services and dishes without a recipe stay Not tracked.
  3. Enter what you have todayStock → Count. The first count sets the opening stock.
  4. Set reorder pointsThe level at which an item should be ordered, and the par to order up to. Mid
  5. Add suppliersOrders → Partners: phone, terms, lead time. Mid
  6. Sell one item and checkRing it up at the till; the ledger shows the sale row.

Inventory · Part 5

When something goes wrong

Stock shows a negative number.

Something was sold that was never received. Selling is never blocked. Record the missing delivery, or count the item to reset it.

The count doesn't match.

Review the differences before posting: a big gap is usually an unrecorded delivery or a sale on the wrong item. Post with a reason; the owner sees it under Shrinkage.

The supplier delivered short.

Receive only what arrived. The order stays Partly received until the rest comes or you close it.

An expired batch was found.

Stock out with reason Expired. With batch tracking, the till warns before selling a short-dated batch. Full

Two tills sold the last bottle while offline.

Both sales stand; stock shows −1 until the next delivery or count. Nothing is lost when the devices sync.
