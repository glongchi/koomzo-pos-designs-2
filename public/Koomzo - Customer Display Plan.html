Plan · validated and built

Customer-facing display — Restaurant & Bar and Retail Flex

A second screen turned towards the customer — the rear screen of an all-in-one POS, a tablet, or a payment terminal — shows each item as it is rung up, the running total, and what to do at payment. The customer checks the bill as it is built, and the cashier stops reading totals aloud.

Validated. All proposals accepted, with two changes: D5 — each touch action (loyalty phone, receipt choice, tipping) is a separate switch per display, so the venue chooses which ones customers see; D12 — available on all tiers, not Mid+.

Side-by-side demoDisplay appRestaurant → Setup → Customer displayRetail Flex → Setup

Goal

Customer

Sees every line, every price change and the total live; knows exactly what to pay and how (cash change, MoMo approval). Can enter a loyalty number or choose a receipt on a touch screen.

Venue

Fewer disputes at the till, faster MoMo approvals, a place for promotions while idle. Works on the shop Wi-Fi with the internet down, like the rest of Koomzo.

Decisions to validate

Proposed answers in bold.

#QuestionProposedWhy
D1How the display gets its dataThe register sends the whole visible state on every change (not item-by-item edits), with a sequence number and a 2-second heartbeat. Prototype: browser channel. Production: the same message over Koomzo Link on the shop Wi-Fi — no internet needed.A dropped message can never leave the screen showing a wrong total: the next snapshot replaces everything.
D2PairingRegister Setup → Customer display → a 4-digit code. The display enters the code once and stays paired. One display per register; a display follows one register.Simple for a cashier, no IT. Built-in rear screens skip pairing (see D9).
D3Screens the customer seesIdle → Order → Payment → Thank you → Idle. Order: every line with quantity, unit price, options and discounts, the newest line highlighted, running total. Payment: amount due large; cash tendered and change; MoMo / Orange Money “approve on your phone” with the countdown; split payments as paid / remaining. Thank you: change due, points earned, then back to idle after 8 s.Matches the till’s own flow, so the customer always sees the same number the cashier sees.
D4What is never shownStaff-only data is filtered out at the register before sending: cost and margin, kitchen notes, void reasons and approvals, staff PINs, other tables. Phone numbers are masked (6•• •• •• 41). A removed line shows briefly struck through, then disappears.Privacy and trust: the customer sees changes to their bill, never internal reasons.
D5Customer can tap (touch displays)Three actions, each switched on or off per display (validated change): loyalty phone entry (on), receipt choice — WhatsApp / SMS / print / none (on), tipping (off). Each arrives at the register as a suggestion the cashier accepts.Useful without slowing the queue; tipping is uncommon locally, so it stays opt-in.
D6Restaurant: whose bill is shownCounter, takeaway and tab orders show while being rung up. A table’s bill appears only when the cashier opens Print bill or Pay for it. Service charge shows as its own line; a split shows only the part being paid. Switch: “Show table orders while ordering”.A counter till moves between tables all evening; a customer at the counter must not see another table’s bill.
D7Retail Flex: sensitive itemsWeighed items show weight × price per kg; variants show size / colour. Age checks show “Staff is checking ID”, never the date of birth. Pharmacy profile: prescription items show “Prescription item” instead of the drug name (on by default for Pharmacy, a switch for others). An attached customer is greeted by first name with their points.Retail sells things customers behind in the queue should not read.
D8Idle screenVenue name and a welcome line, plus up to 3 promo slides. If the Signage module is on, pick one of its playlists instead.Reuses Signage for venues that already have it; nothing new to learn.
D9Hardware supported(a) Built-in rear screen on an all-in-one POS: Open on second screen from the register, no pairing. (b) Separate tablet on the Wi-Fi, paired. (c) Android payment terminal with a screen, running the display app. Three layouts: landscape 10–15" (lines left, total right), portrait 7–8" (stacked), small 4–6" terminal (total first, last item only).Covers the full-suite hardware without a separate product per device.
D10When the link dropsAfter 5 s without a heartbeat the display greys the total and shows “Reconnecting…”. It never shows an old total as current. Reconnect picks up the latest snapshot.A stale total on a customer screen is worse than none.
D11LanguageSet per display — Français (default) or English, independent of the staff language. Dish and product names stay as entered.The cashier and the customer often prefer different languages.
D12Where it is switched onNew capability “Customer display” for Restaurant & Bar and Retail Flex, on all tiers (validated change), in the Control Centre like every other capability.Same on/off mechanism as the rest of the suite; Lite venues don’t see it.

Staff side

Setup → Customer display

Pairing code and status (paired · last seen), Open on second screen, Show test screen, language, welcome line, up to 3 slides or a Signage playlist, table rule (Restaurant), Rx privacy (Retail), tipping, unpair.

On the register

A small status dot in the order header (display connected / reconnecting). Customer suggestions (loyalty phone, receipt choice) appear as a one-tap Accept / Ignore strip — never applied silently.

Message contract

One snapshot type from the register, one event type back. Everything staff-only is removed before the snapshot is built.

Register → display · CfdSnapshot
{ v: 1, seq, at, registerId, venue, lang, phase: 'idle' | 'order' | 'pay' | 'done', order: { no, context, customer: {first, points}|null, lines: [{ id, name, detail, qty, unitLabel, unit, amount, disc, img, removed }], subtotal, discount, svc, tax, taxLabel, total, items, lastId }, pay: { due, paid, remaining, method, state, phoneMasked, expiresAt, tendered, change }, done: { change, points } }
Display → register · CfdEvent
{ type: 'loyaltyPhone' | 'receipt' | 'tip' | 'hello' | 'bye', value, displayId, at } heartbeat: snapshot repeated every 2 s (same seq) — display drops seq ≤ last
Never in a snapshot
cost, margin, stock, kitchen notes, void reasons, approver, staff PIN, other tables / held tickets, full phone numbers, Rx drug names (when Rx privacy is on)

Acceptance checklist

After building, each line is checked and marked Pass or Fail with notes.

  • A1Customer display capability exists for Restaurant & Bar and Retail Flex on all tiers and is visible in the Control Centre.
  • A2Register Setup → Customer display: pair code, paired display status, Open on second screen, test screen, settings (language, idle message, slides, table rule, tipping, Rx privacy).
  • A3Display page: enter code → paired; survives reload; unpair from either side.
  • A4Idle screen with venue name, welcome line and rotating slides (or a Signage playlist when Signage is on).
  • A5Restaurant: adding, changing quantity, discounting and removing lines updates the display within a second; newest line highlighted; removed line struck through briefly.
  • A6Restaurant: table orders appear only at Print bill / Pay (default); counter / takeaway / tab orders appear while ringing; service charge line shown.
  • A7Retail Flex: scanned items, variants, weighed items (kg × price/kg), line discounts and attached customer (first name + points) shown.
  • A8Retail Flex: age-check step shows “Staff is checking ID”; Pharmacy prescription items show “Prescription item”.
  • A9Payment: amount due; cash tendered and change; MoMo / Orange Money pending with masked number and countdown, then approved / declined; split payments as paid / remaining.
  • A10Thank you: change and points; returns to idle after 8 s, or at once when the next order starts.
  • A11No staff-only data in any message sent to the display (checked in the message itself, not just on screen).
  • A12Touch actions: each of loyalty phone, receipt choice and tipping can be switched on/off per display; enabled ones reach the register as an accept / ignore suggestion; defaults loyalty + receipt on, tipping off.
  • A13Heartbeat: display greys and shows Reconnecting after 5 s without a message; recovers on the next snapshot.
  • A14Layouts: landscape, portrait and small-terminal render correctly; text readable at 1 m (total ≥ 48 px on landscape).
  • A15Display language FR / EN per display, independent of the register.
  • A16A side-by-side demo page (register + display) for testing without two devices.
  • A17Guides (FR + EN) for Restaurant and Retail Flex explain pairing and what customers see.
  • A18No console errors on Restaurant & Bar, Retail Flex, the display page and the demo page.

Out of scope (v1)

Card / NFC payment on the terminal itself — the terminal shows the bill and MoMo status; taking card payments needs the acquirer’s SDK.
Signature capture and customer ratings.
One display for several registers (queue-style) — Queue Management already covers calling customers.
Real network transport — the prototype uses an in-browser channel; production plugs the same messages into Koomzo Link.
