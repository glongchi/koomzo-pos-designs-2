Gym · Part 5

When something goes wrong

The internet is down at the 06:00 peak.

Keep scanning. The tablet decides every check-in itself; renewals by MoMo wait to be sent, cash works now.

A member says they paid.

Members → their history shows every payment with its reference. A MoMo still waiting to send shows as such.

A company's seats are full.

The desk sees 12 of 12 seats. Add a seat on the corporate account, or sell a personal plan. Mid

A trainer disputes their commission.

Trainers → their sessions, each signed and dated, with the share applied. Full

The turnstile is offline.

Admit at the desk; the turnstile reconnects by itself. Turnstile hardware — available soon
