Inventory — simplification review & work plan

A read of the 22 reference screens against what we have built, and a proposal for the shape the module should take. The goal set in the brief is simplicity and usability for non-technical operators — so the measure applied throughout is how many decisions a shopkeeper must make before their first item is in stock. Nothing here is implemented yet; this document is for your decisions.

1 · The verdict in one line

The reference app's navigation model is better than ours. Its screen count is much worse. Adopt the first, reject the second.

30
Reference destinations
10 top-level entries, 9 of them expanding into sub-menus. A four-item shop navigates the same tree as a distributor.
9
Ours today
Overview, Items, Stock, Buy, Counts, Move, Reports, Vendors, Setup — flat, but still eight decisions before the first task.
3
Proposed, Lite
Items, Stock, Setup. Five at Standard. Everything else is a capability that appears when switched on.

What their model gets right — adopt these four things

Verbs, not entities

Stock In · Stock Out · Move Stock · Adjust Stock. An operator does not think "I need to create a goods receipt document"; they think "stock arrived". Naming the menu after the action is the single biggest usability win in those screens, and it is why you responded to it.

Every verb lands in one ledger

Transactions is a single chronological list of everything the four verbs produced, in list–detail. The operator posts, then sees the posting appear. That closes the loop we currently leave open — our four verbs write to four different places.

Colour carries the verb

Blue in, coral out, amber move, green adjust — applied to the form title, the ledger row icon and the total. Learnable in one shift, and it maps cleanly onto tokens we already have (--kz-info, --kz-discount, --kz-warning, --kz-success).

One "Partner" instead of two lists

The same field names the supplier on a receipt and the customer on an issue. For a shop where the same wholesaler is sometimes a buyer, two separate contact books is an administrative tax with no payoff. Worth taking.

Where it is over-built — do not carry these

ObservedCost to a non-technical user
Four verbs are four separate screens with near-identical formsThe forms differ by two rows out of eight. Four layouts to learn, four places for our styling to drift apart, four code paths to regress.
Two ways to do the same job. Stock In vs Purchases → Receive; Adjust Stock vs Inventory CountThe user cannot tell which is correct, so they pick the shorter one and the order stays open forever. This is the most damaging pattern in the set.
Reports split into 5 screens (Summary, Past Quantity, Inventory, Sales, Custom Analytics) — four of the five are empty statesNavigating to discover there is nothing to see. One screen with a report picker shows the same thing in one tap and never renders an empty shell.
Data Center as a peer of daily work — Items, Bundles, Locations, Attributes, Partners, Price ListsReference-data admin is a once-a-quarter job sitting permanently beside a ten-times-a-day job. It belongs in Setup.
Item form carries five concerns in one scroll — identity, custom attributes, pricing, photo, opening quantity — with a green Generate button competing against the blue SaveA phone user scrolls past three sections they will never use to reach the quantity field, which is the only reason they opened the form. Two colours of primary button on one screen means neither reads as the primary.
A completion meter and a help "?" on every titlePersistent chrome that does not change with the task. Ours already has a better home for this in Home.

2 · Proposal — the shape I recommend

2.1 · Navigation: five entries at Standard, three at Lite

The verb menu you liked, but the verbs live inside Stock as the actions that feed its ledger rather than as separate destinations. The operator still sees four clearly named buttons — they are just on one screen with the result.

EntryHoldsLiteStandardPro
ItemsCatalogue, list–detail. Per-item stock by location in the pane.●●●
StockThe ledger + four action buttons: Stock in · Stock out · Move · Adjust. Counts join as a fifth at Pro.●●●
OrdersPurchases · Sales · Returns as tabs on one screen, not three nav entries.—●●
ReportsOne screen, report picker. Valuation, movement, low stock, turnover.—●●
SetupLocations, reasons, partners, attributes, price lists, capability switches.●●●
Overview / Vendors / Counts / Move as nav entries all retire. Overview's numbers move to Home where the operator already starts. Vendors becomes Setup → Partners plus a tab in Orders. Counts and Move become actions on Stock. The rail goes from nine to five without losing a single capability — they change address, not existence.

2.2 · One movement sheet with four presets

This is the core recommendation. The four verbs are one operation — a movement — differing only in where stock comes from, where it goes, and what the quantity column means.

Stock in
One location. Partner = supplier. Qty is added.
Stock out
One location. Partner = customer. Qty is removed.
Move
From → To. No partner. Two postings, one document.
Adjust
One location. Qty is the new count, not a delta. Reason required.

Everything else is shared: date (defaults to now), the item rows with on-hand shown beside each, the note field, Submit / Save draft. So the sheet is written once, takes a kind, and the header colour, the location control and the quantity column header switch on it. Four buttons on the Stock screen open it preset — the user experiences four tasks; we maintain one form.

One thing their Adjust screen gets wrong and we should not repeat. The quantity column is labelled "New Quantity" and takes an absolute figure, while Stock In's identical-looking column takes a delta. Same visual position, opposite meaning, no warning. Our sheet must label the column per kind and show the resulting on-hand inline (12 → 9) so the arithmetic is never in the operator's head. Their detail pane already does this well — 0 → −100; the entry form should too.

2.3 · The Stock screen

1
Header: location picker, date range, four action buttons
Stock in · Stock out · Move · Adjust. On a phone these collapse into one New movement split button whose menu is the four verbs — the 44px targets stay honest.
2
Body: the ledger, list–detail
Rows read as the reference ones do — verb + tint, when & who, 3 items / 123, partner, first item name, From/To chip. Our existing .mdgrid/.mdpane contract handles the mobile push.
3
Detail: the document, with before → after per line
Header in the verb's colour, the four facts (location, date, partner, reference), then the lines with On hand and Change columns and a signed total. Footer actions pinned.
4
A post selects its own document
Submit closes the sheet, the new row appears at the top of the ledger already selected, and the toast names what was written. The operator sees the consequence of the button without navigating.

2.4 · The item form — your restriction, made concrete

You asked that the item form be restricted to regular stock items, with bundles and services authored elsewhere. Agreed, and the mechanism matters: remove the Type selector entirely rather than hiding options inside it.

New item — the whole form

Photo · Name · Sell price · Cost · Opening quantity + location · Barcode (scan or generate). Six fields, no scroll on a phone, one primary button.

SKU is generated silently and shown as a quiet line under the name, editable behind Edit identity. A shopkeeper naming their first item should not have to learn what a SKU is.

What appears later, and only when earned

Reorder point, par level and supplier appear once Purchasing is on. Attributes appear once an attribute is defined in Setup. Lot and expiry appear once Lot tracking is on. Each is a section that materialises, not a disabled field.

This is the IV_CAPS / IV_LITE spine we already have — the form should read it rather than render everything and hide.

Services, bundles and non-stock get their own entry points. Add item ▾ on the Items header: the button itself adds a stock item; the caret offers Service, Bundle, Non-stock — each only present if its capability is on, so a Lite shop sees a plain button with no caret at all. A Type dropdown forces every user to read four options and choose; a split button lets 95% tap and never learn the other three exist.

2.5 · Tier map

Tier 1
Lite
A market stall or single shop. One location, no orders, no counts.
  • Items (stock only)
  • Stock in / out / adjust
  • The ledger
  • Setup: reasons
Tier 2
Standard
Two or more locations and a supplier relationship.
  • + Move stock
  • + Locations
  • + Orders (purchases, sales, returns)
  • + Low stock alerts
  • + Reports
Tier 3
Pro
Stock is audited and costed, not just tracked.
  • + Counts & cycle programmes
  • + Bundles, services
  • + Attributes, price lists
  • + Lots & serials
  • + Shared inventory link
Tiering must be a capability flag, never a removed route. We learned this in the master–detail regression: a screen that is not rendered during a change is a screen nobody verifies. One flag read in one place, every screen asks the same question — which is exactly what window.KZ.on('inventory', cap) already does.

5 · Six decisions I need from you

  1. Do the four verbs stay as nav entries, or become buttons on Stock?
    You said you liked the simplistic menu of Stock In / Out / Move / Adjust. My recommendation is buttons on the Stock screen, not rail entries — same four words, same prominence, but the operator sees the ledger update. If you would rather keep them in the rail, say so: it costs four rail slots and I will design for it.
  2. Is stock allowed in without a purchase order?
    Our ACTIONS.md says yes — two legitimate arrival paths. The reference app agrees. But it is the pattern behind "two ways to do the same job", so the mitigation is that Stock in offers to attach an open order when one exists for that partner. Confirm both paths stay.
  3. One Partner list, or separate suppliers and customers?
    Recommend one list with role tags. Cheaper to maintain, and a partner who both supplies and buys is common in this market. Cost: purchase and sales reporting must filter by role rather than by table.
  4. Where do Counts live?
    Recommend a fifth verb on Stock at Pro tier, with cycle programmes in Setup. The alternative is its own entry — defensible, because a count is a multi-day job with its own state, unlike the other four which post instantly.
  5. Is Move Stock in Lite?
    Recommend no — Lite is one location, so Move has nothing to move between, and it would be a button that always errors. It arrives with the second location, automatically.
  6. Excel import — everywhere, or only Items?
    The reference app puts Import from Excel on every form. Recommend Items and Stock in only. Import on an adjustment form is how a mis-mapped column silently rewrites a hundred quantities.
On the reference app generally. What I have taken from those screens is structural — the verb vocabulary, the one-ledger model, the row anatomy, the before→after columns. The visual execution stays entirely Koomzo: purple primary, #f4f6f9 canvas, Ionicons, 10px radii, our type scale and our tabular figures. The result should read as Koomzo solving the same problem, not as a reskin.
